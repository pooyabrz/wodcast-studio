Your camera, your overlay and your audience.

LOCAL NETWORK
01

OBS Browser Source

Add your camera first, then place the transparent overlay above it.

1920 x 1080 30 FPS Transparent
  1. In OBS, add a Browser Source.
  2. Paste the URL and set the source dimensions.
  3. Keep it above the camera in your source list.
  4. Disable source shutdown when hidden.
02

Zoom Virtual Camera

Send the complete OBS scene to your meeting as a camera.

  1. Select Start Virtual Camera in OBS.
  2. Choose OBS Virtual Camera in Zoom.
  3. Select your microphone in Zoom.
  4. Share computer audio separately for sound cues.
1080p is the overlay canvas size. Zoom's received resolution depends on the account, settings and connection.
03

Your phone as a remote

Connect your phone and computer to the same trusted Wi-Fi network.

Use the LAN launcher to enable phone access. Enter the computer's address on the phone, then pair with the six-digit code.
04

Before going live

  • Try start, pause, resume and reset.
  • Check logo and text over your camera.
  • Join Zoom from a second device.
  • Verify the received picture and sound.
  • Save and export your layout.
The saved layout survives a server restart. The timer starts safely in Ready instead of resuming an old session.
YOUR NEXT GREAT SESSION

A simple routine.

Set it up once. Make every workout your own.

1. Start the studio

Install a supported Node.js LTS release, version 22 or later. Unzip the project and run the launcher for your operating system.

node server.mjs

For a phone on your private network:

node server.mjs --lan

Keep the server running during your session.

2. Prepare your workout

Choose a timer preset or enter your own times. Apply timer settings, edit the workout text, then save the layout.

AMRAP: 12-minute time cap. EMOM: ten 60-second rounds. TABATA: eight 20/10-second cycles, including the final rest.

Rounds count timed sections, not an athlete's completed repetitions.

3. Run the session

The lead-in runs before the workout. Work and rest phases switch automatically. Pause preserves the remaining time.

Reset before changing timer settings. Content, colors and visibility can be adjusted during the session.

Enable sound cues on the streaming computer. Only one selected panel plays them.

4. Keep your setup

Save the active layout on the local server. Export a portable file with its uploaded logo.

Back up the whole project folder, including data/. The readable source and development guide are included.

The standalone preview saves in its browser. OBS and phone control use the full server version.
